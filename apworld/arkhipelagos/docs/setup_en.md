# arkhipelagos setup guide

## What you need

- eidos with the arkhipelagos mod.
- Your own Pokemon Black or White cartridge dump, as eidos asks for.
- For the host: Archipelago 0.6.8 or newer, with `arkhipelagos.apworld` installed.

## Install the apworld (host)

Put `arkhipelagos.apworld` in the `custom_worlds` folder of your Archipelago install, or open it with the
Archipelago Launcher. Then generate as usual. The game names are "Pokemon Black (arkhipelagos)" and
"Pokemon White (arkhipelagos)".

## Make your player file

In eidos, open the arkhipelagos mod, choose Archipelago, set your name and options, and save the player file.
Send it to the host. You can also write it by hand from the template the Archipelago Launcher makes.

## Connect

In eidos, type the room link (for example `https://archipelago.gg/room/...`) or `host:port`, and your slot name.
eidos connects through the metaxy relay. Your world data arrives at once; items you receive while away arrive
when you come back.
