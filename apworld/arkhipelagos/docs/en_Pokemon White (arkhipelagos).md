# Pokemon White (arkhipelagos)

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) lists every option. In eidos, the arkhipelagos mod
writes the player file for you.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every item location of Pokemon White (item balls, hidden items, gifts, gym badges and their TMs) can hold any
item of any game in the multiworld. The arkhipelagos randomizers can also change wild, static, gift, trade,
starter and trainer Pokemon, evolutions, TM and HM compatibility, types, base stats, moves and the type chart.
The logic follows the game's own: badges, HMs, key items, story events, and the Pokemon you can catch.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Ghetsis (the default) or the champion Alder, as the goal option says.

## How do I play?

You play in eidos, the native PC port of Pokemon Black and White, with the arkhipelagos mod. There is no patch
file: the mod gets everything it needs from the server when you connect, through the metaxy relay.
See the setup guide.
